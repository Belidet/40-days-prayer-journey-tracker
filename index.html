The Sacred 40-Day Rule

In Holy Orthodox Tradition, 40 days represents a sacred period of purification, spiritual transformation, and unwavering prayer. From Moses on Mount Sinai and Elijah's journey to Horeb, to our Lord Jesus Christ's 40-day fast in the wilderness, this period forms a spiritual ladder. Together, Belidet, Ephi, Seli, and Ermi embark on this 40-day rule from September 11, 2026 to October 20, 2026, acquiring peace of heart through unceasing prayer.
